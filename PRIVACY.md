# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Publisher: Erhan Erden

Simulator Login is an instruction-only plugin. It does not run an external service, create a developer-operated account, collect telemetry, transmit analytics, or store user data on behalf of the developer.

The plugin directs the user's chosen coding agent to interact with a simulator or emulator available in the user's own environment. Test credentials and screenshots may be processed by that agent and by the local tools or third-party services the user has enabled. Their handling is governed by the user's agreements and settings with those providers.

The skill instructs agents not to print passwords, place credentials in source files, retrieve unknown credentials, or retain passwords in device clipboards. Users remain responsible for providing appropriately scoped test accounts and avoiding production secrets when a local or staging account is sufficient.

Questions or privacy concerns can be submitted through the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/erhanrdn/simulator-login-skill/issues). Do not include secrets or personal data in public issues.
